# Purpose

The Product Detail page is the most important conversion page in the Nomad ecosystem.

Unlike a traditional e-commerce product page, Nomad should present products as part of an adventure lifestyle.

The page should answer four questions immediately:

• What is this product?

• Why should I choose it?

• Can I buy or rent it?

• Is it suitable for my next adventure?

The experience should feel premium, informative, editorial, and trustworthy.

# UX Principles

The Product Detail page should feel like reading a premium outdoor equipment review rather than browsing a catalog.

Photography should dominate.

Specifications should be easy to scan.

Buying and renting should feel equally important.

Users should confidently decide whether the product is suitable for their next adventure without leaving the page.

Every interaction should reinforce the Nomad brand values:

• Premium

• Minimal

• Calm

• Trustworthy

• Nature Inspired

• Adventure Focused

The page should encourage exploration, educate users about the equipment, and provide a seamless path to purchase or rental through elegant layouts, generous whitespace, immersive photography, and subtle interactions.
