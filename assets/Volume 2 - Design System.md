# NOMAD UI DESIGN BIBLE

## Volume 2 — Design System (v1.0)

**Project:** Nomad Outdoor Gear Platform

**Document Type:** Design System

**Purpose:**
This document defines the visual language, layout system, spacing, colors, typography, iconography, and reusable design rules that ensure every screen within the Nomad ecosystem feels consistent, premium, modern, and inspired by nature.

---

# 1. Design Principles

Every UI should follow these five principles.

## Simplicity

Remove unnecessary visual noise.

Every component should have a purpose.

---

## Consistency

Spacing, typography, colors, shadows, and interaction should remain identical across every page.

---

## Readability

Content should always be easy to scan.

Large typography.

Comfortable spacing.

High contrast.

---

## Nature First

Photography is the hero.

UI exists to support the photography.

Never compete with it.

---

## Premium Experience

Every interaction should feel carefully crafted.

No flashy animations.

No excessive gradients.

No clutter.

# 31. Design Do's

✔ Use generous whitespace.

✔ Keep photography large.

✔ Maintain consistent spacing.

✔ Use premium typography.

✔ Prioritize readability.

✔ Keep interactions simple.

✔ Use subtle animations.

✔ Maintain visual hierarchy.

✔ Use realistic outdoor photography.

✔ Design for calm and focus.

---

# 32. Design Don'ts

✖ Do not use neon colors.

✖ Do not use heavy gradients.

✖ Do not use excessive shadows.

✖ Do not use cluttered layouts.

✖ Do not overuse animations.

✖ Do not use more than two fonts.

✖ Do not use cartoon illustrations.

✖ Do not use busy backgrounds.

✖ Do not create inconsistent spacing.

✖ Do not overwhelm users with choices.
