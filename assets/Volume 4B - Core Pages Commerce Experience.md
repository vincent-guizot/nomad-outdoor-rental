# Purpose

The Commerce Experience is where users discover products, browse outdoor gear, compare equipment, and explore curated collections.

Unlike traditional e-commerce websites, Nomad should feel like browsing an outdoor magazine rather than scrolling through a marketplace.

Photography should always lead the experience.

Products should feel curated rather than mass-listed.

Whitespace should create a premium shopping atmosphere.

Every page should encourage exploration.

# UX Goals

The Commerce Experience should feel less like a traditional online store and more like exploring a beautifully curated outdoor adventure magazine.

Users should discover products naturally through immersive photography, thoughtful collections, and intuitive browsing tools.

Every page should encourage exploration while maintaining a calm, premium, and editorial visual style consistent with the Nomad brand.
